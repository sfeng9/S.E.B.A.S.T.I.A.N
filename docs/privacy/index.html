S.E.B.A.S.T.I.A.N.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

S.E.B.A.S.T.I.A.N. is a local-first, open-source personal voice assistant. This policy explains what data the software accesses, why it accesses that data, and the controls available to you.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the S.E.B.A.S.T.I.A.N. software and its optional Google integrations. The software is designed to run on the user's own Windows computer. It does not operate a hosted user-account service or a remote user-data database.

2. Information the software handles

Core assistant features may process microphone audio, speech transcripts, conversation context, notes, tasks, lists, settings, and integration results. These are processed locally on the computer running Sebastian, except when a selected integration must contact its external service.

If you enable Google authorization, Sebastian may access:

  • Gmail messages and metadata, read-only: used to find and summarize email that you ask Sebastian to retrieve. Sebastian does not use Gmail access to send, modify, or delete messages.
  • Google Calendar events: used to display, create, update, or delete events at your direction and to synchronize event reminders to Sebastian's local reminder system.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to perform the assistant feature you requested, maintain local context, or provide an enabled background function such as calendar reminder synchronization. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training a shared artificial-intelligence model.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally in the project's ignored secrets/google directory. Local notes, tasks, lists, reminders, settings, and conversation state may remain on your computer until you delete them. Retrieved Gmail and Calendar results are passed to the local assistant for the requested operation and are not uploaded to a Sebastian-operated server.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Sebastian does not sell Google user data or other personal information. It does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Data is sent to an external provider only when required by an integration you enabled, such as Google APIs, Open-Meteo, Home Assistant, or a configured web-search provider, and only for the related feature.

6. Google API Limited Use

S.E.B.A.S.T.I.A.N.'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Security

The project excludes OAuth client secrets, authorization tokens, local databases, and other private runtime data from version control. Users are responsible for protecting their Windows account, project directory, network, and any external-service credentials they configure.

8. Your choices and deletion controls

Google integrations are optional. You can stop access at any time by disabling the integration, deleting its local token file, or revoking Sebastian from your Google Account connections. You can delete locally stored assistant data directly from your computer. Revoking access prevents future Google API requests but does not automatically delete local notes or tasks that you created separately.

9. Children's privacy

Sebastian is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly operate a service that collects children's personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Sebastian's features change. Material updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or security concerns can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
