What Sebastian does

Sebastian combines local speech processing with focused integrations for practical, everyday requests.

01

Talk naturally

Wake-word activation, automatic end-of-speech detection, local speech-to-text, and spoken responses.

02

Stay organized

Local notes, tasks, lists, contextual memory, calendar events, and advance reminders.

03

Know what is current

Time, date, weather, and web search tools provide current information when requested.

04

Control your space

Home Assistant integration supports approved device and scene controls on your own network.

Google account access

Used only when you ask

Google authorization is optional. Sebastian requests the minimum access needed for its Gmail and Calendar features.

Gmail

Read-only access lets Sebastian find and briefly summarize messages you request. It cannot send, edit, or delete email.

Google Calendar

Calendar event access lets Sebastian view, create, update, or delete events at your direction and synchronize local reminders.

Local handling

Authorization tokens and retrieved account data are handled on the computer running Sebastian. The project does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
